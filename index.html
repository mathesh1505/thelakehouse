Nearby Attractions

The best of Kodaikanal is right at your doorstep from The Lake House.

6.4 KM
Vattakanal Waterfalls

A stunning multi-tiered waterfall hidden in the misty Palani Hills. The short trek through dense shola forests makes it an adventure, and the roaring falls are a photographer's dream.

6.4 KM away Google Maps
4.0 KM
Kodaikanal Lake

The crown jewel of Kodaikanal — a star-shaped man-made lake surrounded by lush greenery and misty hills. Enjoy boating, cycling along the lakeside path, or simply soaking in the serene views.

4.0 KM away Google Maps
3.5 KM
Coaker's Walk

A scenic 1 km clifftop promenade built in 1872 with breathtaking views of the plains 2,000 m below. On clear mornings, you can spot Madurai city — a must-do walk for every visitor.

3.5 KM away Google Maps
10 KM
Pine Forest

A magical corridor of tall silver oak and pine trees casting dramatic shafts of light. Perfect for morning walks, cycling, and quiet picnics surrounded by cool mountain air and the scent of pine.

10 KM away Google Maps
3.3 KM
Bryant Park

A beautifully maintained botanical garden with over 325 varieties of roses, rare orchids, and exotic trees. The annual flower show held here draws visitors from across India every spring.

3.3 KM away Google Maps
8.6 KM
Guna Cave

Famous from the Bollywood film "Guna", these dramatic rock formations and deep crevices sit atop Devil's Kitchen — a misty, mysterious spot that blends natural wonder with cinematic history.

8.6 KM away Google Maps
4.6 KM
Upper Lake Viewpoint

One of Kodaikanal's finest panoramic viewpoints offering a sweeping bird's-eye view of the star-shaped lake and the surrounding misty valleys — especially magical at sunrise and sunset.

4.6 KM away Google Maps
