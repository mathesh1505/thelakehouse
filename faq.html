Check-in & Check-out
Check-in time is 12:00 PM (noon) and check-out time is 10:30 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available on request, subject to availability. Please call us at +91 78450 66388 to arrange.
Yes, early check-in and late check-out can be arranged based on room availability. Please contact us at least 24 hours in advance at +91 78450 66388 or via WhatsApp. Additional charges may apply for late check-out beyond 1:00 PM.
All guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in. Accepted documents: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving Licence, or Voter ID. Foreign nationals must present their Passport and valid Visa.
Breakfast & Dining
Yes! Complimentary breakfast is included with every room at no extra charge. It is served fresh every morning as our way of making sure you start each day of your trip perfectly.
Breakfast is served between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM. In-room service is also available throughout the day for meals and beverages.
Yes, we accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Please inform us of your dietary needs at booking or at least 24 hours before arrival.
Rooms & Booking
We offer 3 room types: Suite with Mountain View (₹2,900/night, max 3 guests), Family Room with Balcony (₹2,400/night), and Quadruple Room with Balcony (₹3,900/night, max 4 guests). All rooms include complimentary breakfast, Wi-Fi, heater, and hot water.
Book through our website using the Book Now button, or call us at +91 78450 66388, or WhatsApp us. We will confirm your booking and send a confirmation to your email.
Yes, children are very welcome. Children under 5 stay free. Extra cots can be arranged on request — please mention this at booking.
Yes, call us at +91 78450 66388 to request changes. Modifications are subject to availability and may affect the room rate.
Facilities & Amenities
Yes, high-speed fibre Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and common areas, completely free of charge.
Yes, free on-premises parking with CCTV surveillance is available for all guests.
Yes, all rooms have an electric heater. Kodaikanal can get cold at night, so you will always be comfortable during your stay.
Location & Transport
5/82 A2, Rifle Range Road, Naidupuram, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101. Search "The Lake House Kodaikanal" on Google Maps. We are 4 KM from Kodaikanal Lake.
Nearest railway station: Kodaikanal Road Railway Station (~80 KM). Nearest airport: Madurai Airport (~120 KM). Taxis and buses are available from both.
